Kraft Board — Material Swatch Reference
========================================

Appearance
----------
Natural uncoated paperboard with visible fibers. Warm, earthy brown tone.
Surface is slightly rough, absorbs ink readily. Creates organic, eco-conscious aesthetic.

Color Values (print-ready)
--------------------------
CMYK:  C=20  M=40  Y=60  K=40  (approximate; varies by stock)
RGB:   R=140 G=100 B=60  (screen preview only)
Pantone: Close match to Pantone 7518 C or 7728 C (consult Pantone Solid Uncoated for closest)

Print Notes
-----------
- Works well with uncoated flexo and gravure.
- Ink holdout is low; expect dot gain and less vibrant solids compared to coated.
- Use dark or earthy ink colors to complement the natural substrate.
- Consider varnish or aqueous coating to protect the surface without hiding the texture.

Sustainability
--------------
- Made from virgin or recycled wood pulp.
- 100% recyclable and biodegradable under industrial composting conditions.
- FSC-certified kraft available for certified supply chains.
- Low-energy manufacturing relative to coated board.
