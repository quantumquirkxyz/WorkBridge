Coated White Board — Material Swatch Reference
===============================================

Appearance
----------
Bright, smooth, opaque paperboard with a clay coating on one side (C1S) or both sides (C2S).
Excellent ink holdout and color vibrancy. Preferred for high-graphic folding cartons and luxury packaging.

Color Values (print-ready)
--------------------------
CMYK:  C=0  M=0  Y=0  K=0  (pure white substrate)
RGB:   R=255 G=255 B=255  (screen preview only)
Pantone: White (Pantone 11-0601 TCX for screen; for print, use uncoated/coated white stock)

Print Notes
-----------
- Offset, digital offset, flexo, and gravure all perform well.
- Solids appear crisp and saturated.
- Can be flood-coated or spot-varnished for premium tactile effects.
- UV coatings add high-gloss or soft-touch finishes.

Sustainability
--------------
- Recyclable if de-inking facilities are available.
- Coating additives may reduce recyclability; check with mill.
- Recycled coated white board available in 10%–100% post-consumer content.
- FSC-certified coated stocks widely available.
