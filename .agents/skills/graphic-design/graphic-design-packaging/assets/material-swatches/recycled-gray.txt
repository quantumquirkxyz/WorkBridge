Recycled Gray Board — Material Swatch Reference
================================================

Appearance
----------
Gray interior with recycled fiber visible. Typically has a lighter recycled liner.
Subdued, natural tone with slightly textured surface. Communicates sustainability values.

Color Values (print-ready)
--------------------------
CMYK:  C=15  M=10  Y=10  K=50  (approximate; varies by recycled content)
RGB:   R=160 G=155 B=145  (screen preview only)
Pantone: Close match to Pantone 7544 C or 7527 C (consult Pantone Solid Uncoated)

Print Notes
-----------
- Ink holdout lower than coated board; expect slightly softer color.
- Use higher ink density or darker Pantone solids for best results.
- Flexo and offset work well; digital print may show more fiber texture.
- Consider uncoated finish to keep the eco aesthetic consistent.

Sustainability
--------------
- High post-consumer recycled content (often 50%–100%).
- 100% recyclable and biodegradable under industrial composting.
- FSC-certified recycled grades available.
- Lower carbon footprint than virgin board.
