# Material Selection

## Paper and Board

| Material            | Thickness Range   | Cost Tier | Print Compatibility          | Sustainability Notes                              |
|---------------------|-------------------|-----------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Kraft (natural)     | 12 pt – 24 pt     | Low       | Uncoated flexo, gravure      | Recyclable, biodegradable, high recycled content  |
| Coated white C1S    | 12 pt – 24 pt     | Low–Mid   | Offset, digital, flexo       | Recyclable; coating affects recyclability         |
| Coated white C2S    | 14 pt – 28 pt     | Mid       | Offset, digital              | Both sides coated; preferred for premium cartons  |
| Recycled gray board | 14 pt – 24 pt     | Low–Mid   | Offset, digital (ink holdout varies) | High post-consumer recycled content, compostable  |
| Clay-coated board   | 12 pt – 22 pt     | Low       | Flexo, offset                | Often used for food contact; recycling challenged  |

**Thickness Units:**
- pt (points): 1 pt = 0.351 mm.
- mm: direct metric measurement.
- gsm (grams per square meter): for paper weight only, not board thickness.

## Plastics

| Material | Typical Use                        | Cost Tier | Print Compatibility                  | Sustainability Notes                      |
|----------|------------------------------------|-----------|--------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| PET      | Beverage bottles, blister film     | Mid       | Flexo, gravure, screen (treated)     | Widely recycled (#1); lightweight          |
| PVC      | Clamshells, blister packs          | Low       | Flexo, screen                        | Recycling limited; phasing out in some regions |
| PP       | Film, containers, caps             | Low–Mid   | Flexo, gravure                       | #5 recyclable; moisture barrier           |
| OPP      | Clear pouch overwrap               | Low       | Flexo, gravure                       | Limited recycling; moisture barrier       |
| Laminates| Multi-layer high-barrier pouches   | High      | Flexo, gravure                       | Recycling difficult; mono-material push   |

## Glass

| Type              | Weight Considerations | Cost Tier | Print Compatibility | Sustainability Notes                          |
|-------------------|-----------------------|-----------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Soda-lime glass   | Heavy; impacts freight | Mid       | Screen, pad print, digital UV | Infinitely recyclable; heavy carbon footprint |
| Borosilicate      | Lighter for same volume | High      | Screen, digital UV  | Recyclable; specialty recycling stream        |
| Colored glass     | Heavier; UV protection for contents | Mid | Screen, laser etching | Recyclable if color-sorted; downcycling risk  |

## Sustainable Options

| Option                    | Description                                              | Cost Tier | Notes                                          |
|---------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|-----------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Post-consumer recycled    | Board or plastic with declared PCR content               | Low–Mid   | Look for FSC or PCR certifications              |
| Compostable (industrial)  | PLA, PBAT, or cellulose-based films certified to EN 13432 | High      | Requires industrial composting facility         |
| Biodegradable             | Oxo-degradable or starch-based films                     | Mid       | Recycling stream contamination risk             |
| Forest Stewardship Council| FSC-certified wood fiber for paper/board                 | Low–Mid   | Ensures responsible forest management           |
| Lightweighting            | Reduce gauge while maintaining performance              | Neutral   | Lowers material cost and transport emissions    |
| Mono-material             | Single-resin structures (e.g., 100% PE)                  | Mid       | Improves recyclability vs. multi-layer laminates |

## Cost Tier Reference

- **Low:** Commodity grades, high-volume runs, standard finishes.
- **Low–Mid:** Recycled or natural stocks, standard plastic films.
- **Mid:** Coated boards, standard laminates, standard glass.
- **High:** Specialty papers, rigid board, metalized laminates, glass.
- **Very High:** Biodegradable films, high-barrier laminates, custom textures.
