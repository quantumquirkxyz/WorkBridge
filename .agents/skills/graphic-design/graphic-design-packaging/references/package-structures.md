# Package Structures

## Folding Carton

**Types:** Regular slotted container (RSC), reverse tuck, tuck end auto-lock, straight tuck.

**Typical Dimensions:**
- Flat size: width × height × depth, expressed in mm or inches.
- Common stock: 12 pt to 24 pt (0.15 mm to 0.30 mm).
- Standard flat sizes: 100×150 mm up to 400×600 mm.

**Material Suggestions:**
- Uncoated kraft for natural/grocery look.
- Coated white C1S or C2S for high-end graphics.
- Recycled content board for sustainability positioning.

**Industry Use Cases:**
- Food retail, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, consumer electronics accessories.

**Dieline Complexity:** Low to moderate. RSC is the simplest; reverse tuck and tuck end auto-lock add tuck flaps and locking mechanisms.

## Rigid Box

**Types:** Set-up box, magnetic closure box, hinged lid box, slip lid.

**Typical Dimensions:**
- Base depth: 25 mm to 150 mm.
- Wall thickness: 1.5 mm to 3.0 mm (chipboard wrapped with paper or leather).
- Lid typically 2–3 mm larger than base for easy fit.

**Material Suggestions:**
- 1.5 mm–2.5 mm grayboard (chipboard) covered with specialty paper, linen, or leather.
- Rigid recycled board for sustainable luxury.

**Industry Use Cases:**
- Luxury cosmetics, jewelry, watches, premium spirits, high-end tech unboxing.

**Dieline Complexity:** High. Requires separate base and lid dielines, precise wrap allowance, and often multiple artwork layers.

---

## Corrugated

**Types:** Regular slotted container (RSC), half-slotted container (HSC), full-overlap slotted container (FOL), telescope box, tray.

**Typical Dimensions:**
- flute sizes: B (3 mm), C (4 mm), E (1.5 mm), F (1 mm).
- Common flat sizes: 200×200 mm up to 1200×800 mm.

**Material Suggestions:**
- B-flute for retail-ready point-of-purchase displays.
- C-flute for shipping and general transport.
- E-flute for lightweight consumer packaging and displays.

**Industry Use Cases:**
- Shipping cartons, e-commerce fulfillment, retail ready-pack, beverage carriers, produce trays.

**Dieline Complexity:** Moderate. RSC and HSC are straightforward; FOL and telescope styles add complexity. Follow FEFCO codes for standardization.

---

## Flexible Packaging

**Types:** Pouch, stand-up pouch (Doypack), sachet, pillow pouch, flat bottom bag.

**Typical Dimensions:**
- Pouch widths: 50 mm to 300 mm.
- Stand-up base gusset: 30 mm to 120 mm.

**Material Suggestions:**
- Laminated films: PET/PE, PET/ALU/PE, OPP/PE, kraft paper/PE.
- Mono-material PE or PP for recyclability targets.

**Industry Use Cases:**
- Snack foods, coffee, dried goods, pet food, personal care sachets.

**Dieline Complexity:** High for structural art because of gussets, zippers, spouts, and heat-seal areas. Layouts are usually repeat patterns.

---

## Blister / Clamshell

**Types:** Blister pack (carded), clamshell (sealed on three sides), slider blister, hang hole.

**Typical Dimensions:**
- Cavity depth: 5 mm to 50 mm depending on product.
- Card size: typically 10–30 mm larger than blister footprint on all sides.

**Material Suggestions:**
- Blister: PET, PVC, or RPET thermoformed film, 0.15 mm to 0.40 mm.
- Card: coated white board 12 pt to 18 pt.

**Industry Use Cases:**
- Small consumer goods, hardware, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, toys.

**Dieline Complexity:** High. Must account for vacuum-formed cavity shape, hang holes, scoring lines, and precise seal areas.

---

## Tube

**Types:** Rigid tube, flexible laminate tube, paper tube.

**Typical Dimensions:**
- Diameter: 20 mm to 100 mm.
- Length: 50 mm to 300 mm.
- Wall thickness: 0.3 mm (flexible) to 1.0 mm (rigid).

**Material Suggestions:**
- Flexible tube: laminated plastic/Aluminum/PE.
- Paper tube: spiral wound kraft or recycled board.

**Industry Use Cases:**
- Toothpaste, paints, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, candles.

**Dieline Complexity:** Moderate for flexible tubes; low for simple paper tubes. Complex closure systems add complexity.

---

## Bottle / Jar

**Types:** Plastic bottle, glass jar, aluminum bottle, wide-mouth jar.

**Typical Dimensions:**
- Capacity: 30 ml to 1000 ml common for consumer products.
- Neck finish: standard diameters (e.g., 24/410, 28/400) for closure compatibility.

**Material Suggestions:**
- PET (water, beverages), HDPE (shampoo, cleaners), PP (food jars), glass (preserves, spirits), aluminum (beer, premium beverages).

**Industry Use Cases:**
- Beverages, personal care, food preserves, sauces, pharmaceuticals.

**Dieline Complexity:** Moderate for label design only; structural design typically handled by container manufacturer. shrinks sleeves add artwork complexity.
