# Regulatory Labels

## European Union (EU)

| Requirement                  | Applies To                      | Placement Notes                          |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| CE marking                   | Products covered by EU directives (electronics, toys, PPE, machinery) | Visible, legible, and indelible on product or packaging |
| Recycling symbols            | Packaging materials (packaging waste) | On packaging; specify material code (e.g., PET 01, PAP 20) |
| Material declarations        | Plastic packaging (PPW Directive) | Code inside triangle, text or symbol      |
| Country of origin labeling   | Many product categories (food, textiles, etc.) | Front panel or back panel depending on product type |
| Ingredients / allergens      | Food, cosmetics, detergents     | Back panel, minimum 1.2 mm type height    |
| Warnings and safety          | Toys, chemicals, electronics     | Pictograms and text on front or back      |
| Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) numbers | Packaging | Brand/size reference on packaging in some member states |

**Common EU label placements:**
- Front: brand, product name, net quantity.
- Back: ingredients, allergens, nutrition table (food), warnings, recycling codes.
- Side/Bottom: EPR identifiers, barcodes.

## United States (US)

### FDA (Food, Drugs, Cosmetics, Medical Devices)

| Requirement                  | Applies To                      | Placement Notes                          |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Statement of identity        | Food, drugs, cosmetics          | Principal display panel (PDP)            |
| Net quantity of contents     | Food, drugs, cosmetics, many consumer goods | PDP, parallel to base, minimum 1/8 inch tall |
| Ingredient declaration       | Food, cosmetics, drugs          | Back or side panel, descending predominance |
| Nutrition facts (food)       | Most packaged foods             | PDP or back panel, standard format        |
| Allergen labeling            | Food, cosmetics (FALCPA)        | Back panel, contain or may contain statement |
| Drug facts / facts for use   | OTC drugs, cosmetics            | Back or side panel                        |
| UPC / barcode                | Most retail packaged goods      | Bottom or back panel                      |
| Country of origin            | Textiles, fur products, autos, some electronics | Front or back, conspicuous and legible |
| CPSC warnings                | Toys, household items, children’s products | Near product view or on visible surface   |

### CPSC (Consumer Product Safety)

| Requirement                  | Applies To                      | Placement Notes                          |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| choking hazard warnings      | Toys, small parts              | Near product view, visible at point of sale |
| Age grading                  | Toys, children’s products       | Back panel                                |
| Safety instructions          | Consumer electronics, furniture  | Back or side panel                        |
| Lead and phthalate warnings  | Children’s products             | Near product view or back panel           |

## General Requirements (Global)

| Requirement                  | Applies To                      | Placement Notes                          |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Country of origin (made in)  | Textiles, fur, autos, some electronics in US; many products globally | Front or back, conspicuous |
| Barcode / GS1-128            | Retail and logistics            | Bottom or back panel, scannable, 2–3 mm quiet zone |
| QR code / Digital link        | Optional; growing adoption      | Front or back; ensure contrast and size (minimum 15×15 mm) |
| Batch / lot code and best before | Food, pharma, cosmetics      | Back or side, embossed or printed         |
| Recycling / disposal symbols  | Packaging (EU and many countries) | On packaging body, not on shrink sleeve only in some regions |
| Hazard symbols (GHS/CLP)      | Chemicals, aerosols             | Back panel, minimum size relative to package |
| Tare weight                  | Bulk, bag-in-box, shipping cartons | Bottom or side panel                    |
| Temperature / storage guidance | Perishables, pharma            | Back or side panel                        |

## Common Label Placements

| Face            | Typical Content                                                                 |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Front panel     | Brand, product name, variant, net weight/volume, key visual, barcode           |
| Back panel      | Ingredients, directions, warnings, nutrition/facts, recycling, country of origin |
| Side panel      | Dimensions, serving suggestions, supplementary ingredients, legal text          |
| Bottom panel    | Tare weight, batch code, lot number, EPR identifiers                            |
| Top/Inner flap  | Promotional codes, secondary messaging, loyalty triggers                        |
