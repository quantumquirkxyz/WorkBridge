# Logo Systems Reference

## Logo Classification

### Wordmark
- Logo composed solely of the brand name in a custom or distinctive typeface.
- Examples: Google, Coca-Cola, FedEx.

### Lettermark
- Logo composed of initials or acronyms.
- Examples: IBM, HBO, NASA.

### Symbol / Logomark
- Iconic symbol without text.
- Examples: Apple, Twitter, Target.

### Combination Mark
- Wordmark paired with a symbol.
- Examples: Adidas, Lacoste, Pizza Hut.

### Emblem
- Text enclosed within a symbol or icon.
- Examples: Starbucks, Harley-Davidson, NFL.

## Logo Variation Patterns

### Layout Variations
- **Horizontal:** wordmark and symbol side by side.
- **Stacked:** wordmark above or below symbol.
- **Lockup:** predefined arrangement for specific contexts.

### Color Variations
- **Full color:** default brand colors.
- **Monochrome:** single color, usually black or white.
- **Reversed:** inverted colors for dark backgrounds.

### Context Variations
- **App icon:** simplified mark for small sizes.
- **Favicon:** extreme simplification for browser tabs.
- **Social profile:** circular or square crop for platform avatars.

## Clear Space Rules
- Define a minimum clear space unit based on the symbol height or letter height.
- Do not place other elements within the clear space.
- Scale the clear space with the logo.

## Minimum Size
- Digital: specify minimum pixel dimensions for web and app use.
- Print: specify minimum physical size for readability.

## Do and Don't Examples
- Do maintain aspect ratio.
- Do use approved color values.
- Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the logo.
- Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or gradients unless explicitly approved.
- Do not place on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast.
