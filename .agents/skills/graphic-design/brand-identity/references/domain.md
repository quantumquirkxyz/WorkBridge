# Brand Identity Domain Reference

## Core Concepts
- **Brand:** the perception and emotional response triggered by a product, service, or organization.
- **Brand identity:** the visible elements of a brand — name, logo, color, typography, imagery — that together identify and distinguish it.
- **Brand strategy:** the plan for how the brand will achieve its goals in the market.
- **Brand equity:** the commercial value derived from consumer perception.
- **Brand architecture:** the structure of brands within an organization (house of brands, branded house, hybrid).

## Logo System Components
- **Primary logo:** the default, full-color logo used in most contexts.
- **Wordmark:** the brand name rendered in a distinctive typeface.
- **Symbol / logomark:** the brand mark used without the wordmark.
- **Monochrome:** single-color version for limited color contexts.
- **Stacked / horizontal:** layout variations for different aspect ratios.

## Color System Roles
- **Primary:** dominant brand color used for logo, headlines, and key actions.
- **Secondary:** supporting color for variety and emphasis.
- **Accent:** highlight color for calls to action and key details.
- **Neutral:** grayscale values for body text, backgrounds, and borders.

## Typography Roles
- **Display:** typeface for headlines and large text.
- **Body:** typeface for paragraphs and long-form reading.
- **Mono:** typeface for code, data, and technical content.

## Imaginary and Iconography
- **Photography style:** consistent treatment of real-world images.
- **Illustration style:** custom-drawn or stylized visuals.
- **Iconography:** simplified symbols for navigation, actions, and status.

## Brand Touchpoints
- Digital: website, app, social media, email.
- Print: business cards, letterhead, packaging, signage.
- Environmental: office, retail, events, vehicles.
- Merchandise: apparel, swag, promotional items.
