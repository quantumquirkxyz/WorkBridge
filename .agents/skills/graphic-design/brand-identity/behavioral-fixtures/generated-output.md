# Behavioral Fixture for Brand Identity

## Expected Output Shape

- Includes brand strategy and positioning summary.
- Describes logo system with variations and clear-space rules.
- Provides color palette with values, formats, and accessibility notes.
- Provides typography system with scale and licensing notes.
- Describes imagery and iconography style.
- Documents usage governance and approval workflow.
- Does not claim production assets were created.

## Prohibited Output

- Placeholder-only completion claims.
- Unstated governance assumptions.
- Generic aesthetic advice not tied to brand strategy.
- Claims that final brand assets were produced without evidence.
