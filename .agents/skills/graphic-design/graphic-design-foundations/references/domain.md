# Graphic Design Domain Reference

## Terminology
- **Bleed:** area beyond trim line that ensures print extends to the edge.
- **Safe zone:** area inside trim where critical content must stay.
- **CMYK:** print color model (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Key/Black).
- **RGB:** screen color model (Red, Green, Blue).
- **Pantone:** standardized color matching system for print.
- **OKLCH:** perceptually uniform color space for digital design.
- **Kerning:** adjustment of space between individual letter pairs.
- **Leading:** vertical space between lines of type.
- **Tracking:** uniform adjustment of space across a range of characters.
- **Hierarchy:** visual ordering that guides the viewer through content.
- **Focal point:** area of highest visual interest.
- **Grid:** underlying structure for alignment and consistency.
- **Visual flow:** path the eye follows through the composition.

## Color Roles
- **Primary:** dominant brand or message color.
- **Secondary:** supporting color for variety and emphasis.
- **Accent:** highlight color for calls to action and key details.
- **Neutral:** grayscale values for text, backgrounds, and borders.

## Production Channels
- **Print:** requires bleed, trim, CMYK or Pantone, 300 PPI minimum.
- **Digital:** RGB or OKLCH, 72–150 PPI, sRGB or Display P3.
- **Outdoor:** large format, high contrast, simplified messaging.
- **Packaging:** dielines, structural templates, material constraints.
