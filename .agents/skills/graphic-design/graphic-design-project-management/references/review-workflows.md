# Review Workflows Reference

## Review Types

### Internal Creative Review
- Participants: design team, creative director.
- Focus: creative quality, brand alignment, technical execution.
- Output: feedback document with revision requests.

### Stakeholder Review
- Participants: project owner, marketing, legal, subject matter experts.
- Focus: message accuracy, compliance, business fit.
- Output: approval or revision requests.

### Brand Review
- Participants: brand team or brand guardian.
- Focus: brand compliance, logo usage, color accuracy.
- Output: brand approval or correction requests.

### Legal Review
- Participants: legal team.
- Focus: copyright, trademark, regulatory compliance.
- Output: legal approval or correction requests.

### Final Approval
- Participants: project owner or designated approver.
- Focus: overall quality and readiness for delivery.
- Output: final sign-off.

## Feedback Formats

### Written Feedback
- Structured document with sections per deliverable.
- Categorized feedback: direction, concept, detail, copy, technical.
- Priority: must change, should change, nice to have.

### Comment Layers
- Comments placed directly on design file.
- Threaded discussions for complex feedback.
- Status per comment: open, resolved, won't fix.

### Markup
- Visual markup on exported preview files.
- Arrow and text annotations.
- Color-coded by feedback type.

## Approval Criteria

### Creative Quality
- Meets design brief objectives.
- Cohesive and polished execution.
- Innovative and on-brand.

### Brand Compliance
- Correct logo usage and clear space.
- Approved color values and typography.
- Prohibited styles avoided.

### Accessibility
- Text contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Information not conveyed by color alone.
- Readable type sizes and line lengths.

### Technical Quality
- Correct resolution and color space.
- No artifacts or compression errors.
- Files named and organized correctly.

## Escalation Path
1. Designer and reviewer resolve at peer level.
2. Creative director resolves design disputes.
3. Project owner resolves timeline or scope disputes.
4. Brand team resolves brand compliance disputes.
5. Legal resolves legal or regulatory disputes.
