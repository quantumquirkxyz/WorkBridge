# Graphic Design Project Management Domain Reference

## Core Concepts
- **Design brief:** written document describing project objectives, audience, message, deliverables, constraints, and success criteria.
- **Design iteration:** repeated cycle of creating, reviewing, and revising design work.
- **Design review:** structured feedback session on design work.
- **Asset handoff:** transfer of final design files and documentation to production or client.
- **DesignOps:** operational discipline for scaling design teams and workflows.

## Project Phases
- **Discovery:** research, interviews, competitive analysis, mood boards.
- **Concept:** initial ideas, sketches, wireframes, mockups.
- **Refinement:** detailed design, feedback integration, iteration.
- **Production:** final files, asset preparation, quality checks.
- **Delivery:** handoff, documentation, knowledge transfer.

## Stakeholder Roles
- **Project owner:** accountable for project success and timeline.
- **Creative director:** responsible for creative quality and brand alignment.
- **Designer:** executes design work.
- **Reviewer:** provides feedback on design work.
- **Approver:** has authority to approve deliverables.
- **Production:** prepares assets for final use.

## Iteration Models
- **Waterfall:** sequential phases with defined handoffs.
- **Agile:** iterative cycles with continuous feedback.
- **Design sprint:** time-boxed intensive design period.
- **Double diamond:** discover, define, develop, deliver.

## Feedback Types
- **Direction:** strategic feedback on overall approach.
- **Concept:** feedback on specific ideas or directions.
- **Detail:** feedback on specific elements or execution.
- **Copy:** feedback on text content.
- **Technical:** feedback on production requirements.

## Quality Dimensions
- **Brand compliance:** adherence to brand guidelines.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG compliance, readability, color contrast.
- **Resolution:** appropriate pixel density and file size.
- **Color accuracy:** correct color space and values.
- **Spelling and grammar:** error-free text.
- **Production readiness:** files in correct formats, layers organized, fonts embedded.

## Handoff Artifacts
- **Source files:** editable files in native application format.
- **Exported files:** final files in delivery formats.
- **Preview files:** low-resolution or web-optimized previews.
- **Documentation:** readme, color values, font list, production notes.
- **Style guide:** brand usage rules and component specifications.
