# Design Brief Templates Reference

## Standard Design Brief

### Project Overview
- Project name and code.
- Client or stakeholder.
- Project owner and creative director.
- Timeline and budget.

### Objectives
- Business objectives.
- Communication objectives.
- Success metrics.

### Audience
- Primary audience description.
- Secondary audience description.
- Audience expectations and pain points.

### Message
- Core message.
- Supporting messages.
- Tone and voice.

### Deliverables
- List of deliverables with formats and specifications.
- Quantity per deliverable.
- Distribution channels.

### Constraints
- Brand guidelines.
- Legal or regulatory constraints.
- Technical constraints.
- Budget constraints.

### Inspiration and References
- Competitor examples.
- Mood boards.
- Style references.

## Social Media Brief

### Platform
- Platform name and specifications.
- Aspect ratio and dimensions.
- File format and size limits.

### Content
- Headline or hook.
- Body copy.
- Call to action.
- Hashtags or tags.

### Visual Direction
- Photography or illustration style.
- Color palette.
- Typography direction.

### Scheduling
- Publication date and time.
- Campaign duration.

## Packaging Brief

### Product
- Product name and description.
- Product dimensions and weight.
- Shelf placement and point-of-sale context.

### Structure
- Box, bottle, pouch, or other format.
- Dimensions and dieline.
- Material and finish.

### Visuals
- Front panel: logo, product name, hero image.
- Back panel: ingredients, instructions, barcode.
- Sides and bottom: legal text, branding.

### Production
- Printer specifications.
- Color profile and proofing.
- Material samples.
