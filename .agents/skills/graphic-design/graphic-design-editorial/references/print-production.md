# Print Production

## Page Sizes

- A4: 210 × 297 mm (8.27 × 11.69 in) — standard for reports, many European books.
- A5: 148 × 210 mm (5.83 × 8.27 in) — common for paperbacks and journals.
- B5: 176 × 250 mm (6.93 × 9.84 in) — popular for literary fiction.
- US Letter: 8.5 × 11 in (215.9 × 279.4 mm) — North American standard.
- US Trade: 6 × 9 in (152.4 × 228.6 mm) — common for nonfiction and literary books.
- Custom trim: specify exact finished size; verify press capability before finalizing.

## Bleed and Safety Margins

- Standard bleed: 3 mm (0.125 in) on all trim edges.
- Larger formats or digital cutting may require 5 mm bleed.
- Safety margin (live area): keep all critical text and images at least 3–5 mm inside trim.
- Crop marks and registration marks sit in the bleed area; do not place content there.

## Color Modes

- CMYK for full-color print; convert all RGB sources before layout.
- Grayscale for black-and-white publications; ensure images have sufficient tonal range.
- Spot colors (Pantone, HKS, RAL) for brand-critical publications; define in swatch panel.
- Rich black for text: C60 M40 Y40 K100 or press-specific recommendation; avoid 100K alone for large black areas.

## Resolution Requirements

- Print images: 300 PPI at final printed size.
- Minimum acceptable print: 250 PPI for images viewed at reading distance.
- Web / digital: 72 PPI or 2× device pixel ratio for responsive images.
- Vector artwork (logos, line art): embed as vector or 1200+ PPI raster.

## PDF Export Settings and Preflight

- PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 for press-ready output; confirm with printer.
- Embed all fonts or outline fonts if licensing restricts embedding.
- Include bleed, trim, and registration marks in PDF export.
- Transparencies: flatten if PDF/X-1a is required; PDF/X-4 preserves live transparency.
- Preflight checks: missing fonts, low-resolution images, overprint issues, CMYK/RGB mismatch, hairline strokes.
