# Copy Layout

## Headline Hierarchy and Readability

- Single headline per creative unless using a carousel or multi-slide format.
- Headline length by format: display ads 3-7 words, social posts 4-8 words, email banners 5-10 words, print headlines 2-6 words.
- Use title case or sentence case consistently across the campaign.
- Place headline in the top third of the creative for feed formats; top third to center for story formats.

## Body Copy Length by Format

| Format | Maximum Characters | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display ad | 90 | Shortest limit; treat as headline extension |
| Social post caption | 125 | On-screen; link in comments for more |
| Story | 50 per screen | Text overlay limit |
| Email banner | 120 | Subhead only; body in email body |
| Print | 30 words | Large format may support more |

## CTA Button Design and Placement

- Single CTA per creative.
- Button label: 1-3 words; action-oriented (Shop Now, Learn More, Sign Up).
- Place CTA in the bottom third for feed formats; middle to bottom third for stories.
- Ensure minimum touch target 44x44 px for mobile formats.
- Use contrasting brand color for CTA background; white text for readability.

## Visual Flow and Eye Tracking

- **Z-pattern** — headline top-left, visual center, CTA bottom-right. Use for landscape and display ads.
- **F-pattern** — strong horizontal headline, supporting vertical elements on the left. Use for web and email.
- **Center-out** — hero image or headline in center, CTA below. Use for square social and story formats.
- Avoid competing focal points; use scale and contrast to establish primary and secondary elements.

## Whitespace and Emphasis

- Minimum 10% margin around all content blocks.
- Use whitespace to separate headline, body, and CTA into distinct visual groups.
- Increase whitespace around the CTA to draw the eye.
- Avoid crowding mobile formats; if content exceeds safe area, split into a carousel or multi-slide sequence.
