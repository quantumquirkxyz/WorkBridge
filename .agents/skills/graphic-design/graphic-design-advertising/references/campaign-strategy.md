# Campaign Strategy

## Campaign Objective Mapping

Align creative direction with the primary objective:

- **Awareness** — high visual impact, minimal copy, bold imagery, broad reach formats (stories, feed posts, billboards).
- **Consideration** — product detail, benefit-led messaging, comparison cues, mid-funnel formats (carousels, leaderboards, sponsored content).
- **Conversion** — single CTA, urgency or offer focus, friction-reduced layout, direct-response formats (search ads, email banners, shoppable posts).

## Target Audience and Persona Alignment

- Define primary and secondary audience segments before selecting visuals.
- Match tone, imagery style, and cultural references to persona demographics, psychographics, and device behavior.
- Document preferred channels and formats per persona in the brief.

## Visual Messaging and Value Proposition

- Lead with the core value proposition in the hero area of the creative.
- Use imagery that demonstrates outcome or emotion, not just product.
- Avoid generic stock when custom photography or illustration reinforces brand distinctiveness.
- Maintain a single message per creative; secondary messages belong in carousel slides or sequential variations.

## A/B Testing Variables

Test one variable at a time for statistically valid results:

- **Headline** — message framing, length, emotional vs rational tone
- **Visual** — product vs lifestyle, color palette, human presence
- **CTA** — button text, color, placement, urgency cues
- **Offer** — discount type, value framing, expiry messaging
- **Audience** — segment, interest cluster, lookalike vs interest targeting

Document hypothesis, sample size, duration, and primary metric before launch.

## Success Metrics and KPIs

| Objective | Primary KPI | Secondary KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Impressions, Reach | Frequency, Video views |
| Consideration | CTR, Engagement rate | Time on landing page, Video completion |
| Conversion | CPA, ROAS | Add to cart, Sign-up rate |

Set benchmarks before campaign start and review at 25%, 50%, and 100% of flight duration.
