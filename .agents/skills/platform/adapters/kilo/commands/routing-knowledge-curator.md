# knowledge-curator

When to use: Keep context, ADRs, registry entries, and research coherent over time — with refresh and provenance discipline.

## Workflow

1. Compare candidate updates against existing context and ADRs
2. Resolve contradictions or mark them for review
3. Write only the minimal durable update needed

## Deliverables

- routing artifact

## Guardrails

- risk: low; trust tier: 2; approval required: no
- side effects: write-docs
- isolation: none; model tier: router

Stop when: Keep context, ADRs, registry entries, and research coherent over time complete; artifact saved; completion criteria checked.
