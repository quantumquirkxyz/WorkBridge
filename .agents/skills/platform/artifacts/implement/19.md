## Why this was not a pure rename

The design document called slice 1 a no-op. It could not be one. `shortlist.test.ts` pinned
the opposite of ADR-0010's policy for a candidate without a persisted profile (silent empty
list, not `503`), and the adapter was collapsing four separate causes behind one `undefined`.
Folding them together would have broken that pin, so the seam now reports the reason and each
consumer maps it — which is also the better interface, because it keeps policy out of the seam.

## Risk remaining

The behaviour change lands in slice 2, where `createDraft` becomes asynchronous and consumes
`decisionFor`. Until then the vulnerability is open. The new reasons are not exercised over
HTTP, and the browser-level and HTTP-layer paths remain outside this slice.
