## Changes

- Better Auth roles `candidate`, `employer`, and `counselor` are read from `session.user.role`.
- Drafts copy declared evidence and match-gap text. They do not invent claims.
- Candidate approval is required before submission.
- Counselor approval is required for a critical gap, an unverified credential, a bias alert, or infeasible transport.
- Employer approval is required before interview progression.
- Other roles cannot submit. Certo tokens, provider keys, and database URLs are rejected.
- Submission is idempotent. Each approval, correction, rejection, and submission appends an `AuditEvent` with actor, role, timestamp, reason, and `traceId`.
- `Dockerfile` uses `node:22-alpine`. `vercel.json` routes to the application handler, and `NEON_DATABASE_URL` is the backup when `DATABASE_URL` is empty.
