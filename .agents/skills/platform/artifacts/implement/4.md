## Risk remaining

Heuristic extraction quality is imperfect: section headers can surface as experiences or
certifications and sentence titles are sometimes truncated (e.g. `Ayudaba en la tienda de mi
familia los`). These are cosmetic issues in the deterministic path, not contract or
classification failures. The optional `tesseract.js` OCR fallback is intentionally uninstalled and
aliased to a stub. Mastra workflow orchestration, candidate preferences (ticket #7) and the safety
plane (ticket #9) remain out of scope.
