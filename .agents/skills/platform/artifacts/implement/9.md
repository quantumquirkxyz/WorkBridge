## Changes

- `packages/domain`: `AgentEnvelope` and `WorkflowRunRequest` JSON Schema 2020-12 contracts. `trace_id` and `idempotency_key` are required and validated with Ajv.
- Guardrails for input, tool results, permissions, and output. They block prompt injection, PII leakage, invalid schemas, invented credentials, and unauthorized tools. Ten fixtures live in `fixtures/guardrails/cases.json`.
- OpenRouter capability chains for extraction, matching, and planning, with token budgets, catalog preflight, and ordered fallback.
- Langfuse ingestion batches, append-only `audit_events`, and a DQL store with `pending`, `success`, `failed`, `rejected`, `retrying`, and `dead_letter`.
- Mastra workflow coordinating Exploration, Profile and Evidence, Market and Matching, and Training. Internal REST/SSE adapter: `POST /internal/workflow/runs`.
